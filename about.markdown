This blog intends to document the progress of my little research projects which span from pure math research, inventing new media for thinking to small hacking projects. However, don't expect my articles to be well-polished and edited. They are more like a dumping ground for half-formed ideas I haven't found the rights words for yet. 

You will see that my math notes don't resemble the logical structure that most textbooks or lectures follow, because I intend to draw more attention to the creative process rather than whether my proofs are correct and rigorous. You will also see that I tend to jump around between different fields, because I believe that most boundaries people set up are imaginary.

Some of my small hacking projects live on their own pages, for example the [Adding Machine Table](/adding-machine-table/), a simulated projected table where you move number cards around and watch them get parsed into groups and summed.

Welcome to my waste basket! Follow me for updates.
